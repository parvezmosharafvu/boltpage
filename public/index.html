Questions

Who pays the fees?

The payer pays exactly the amount shown. A service commission is deducted from what you receive, and you see it on every payment.

What are the limits?

Each payment can be between $10 and $1,000.

Why does my account need approval?

To keep the platform safe, an admin reviews every new account before it can receive payments.

How do I get my money?

Request a withdrawal of your available balance from your dashboard.
