What we collect

Account holders: name, email, phone, optional Telegram username, payment and withdrawal records. Payers: nothing beyond the payment itself; IP addresses are hashed with a secret salt for rate limiting and not stored in clear.

What payers see

A payment page shows only the display name and note the account holder chose. It never shows their email, balance or other payments.

Retention

Financial records are kept as required for accounting. Rate-limit counters are deleted after one day.
